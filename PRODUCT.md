# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Public walk-up customers at a self-service koperasi kiosk, paying via Midtrans QRIS on a shared device or their own phone. Secondary user: the admin who manages the product catalog (stock, prices, active flags). Buyers are in a hurry, often one-handed on mobile; the job is pick items, set quantities, pay, done.

## Product Purpose

"Kopi Kita" koperasi self-service ordering: browse products, enter grams/pieces, pay by QRIS, see confirmation. Stock decrements on settlement so the catalog reflects reality. Success = a first-time user completes a purchase without help, and the admin keeps the catalog accurate in under a minute.

## Brand Personality

Warm, communal, playful. Poster-like warmth (cream surface, deep brown, orange accent), rounded shapes, casual Indonesian copy. The brand mark is the coffee cup; the shop sells arbitrary goods.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboard: no blue-gray enterprise look, no hero-metric templates, no identical icon-card grids. Not corporate e-commerce (Tokopedia/Shopee clone). Not sterile white/gray minimalism; warmth is part of the brand.

## Design Principles

- Speed over ceremony: the shortest path from "I want this" to paid wins; every extra step is a leak.
- Stock honesty: availability is visible before the user commits; out-of-stock is unmissable but not alarming.
- Warmth without noise: the poster aesthetic lives in color, shape, and copy, not in decorative elements competing with the task.
- One-handed mobile first: tap targets generous, primary action always reachable, forms minimal.
- Admin is a tool, not a showcase: dense, predictable, fast; the same warm vocabulary, zero flourish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on text. Out-of-stock state conveyed by more than color alone (label + disabled). Touch targets ≥44px. Indonesian copy throughout.
